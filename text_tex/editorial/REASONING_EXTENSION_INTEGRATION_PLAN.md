# Integration checklist: upcoming Bolsen and Corbacho reasoning annotation

**Status (15 October 2026): planned, not yet observed.** Do not report category prevalence, direction, coder agreement, or predictive fits until an actual annotation analysis exists.

The behavioral analysis for both new studies is already included in the editorial manuscript. The historical reasoning and local hidden-state tracks cover only Hassner and Savary.

## Future insertion points

1. Analytical Strategy > Annotation of verbalized reasoning: explain experiment-specific coding adaptations and validation, with explicit common-category continuity across the four domains. Cite the reviewed final codebooks rather than preliminary labels.
2. Results > Verbalized reasoning: add a subsection comparing prevalence and signed argumentative direction across prompt conditions *within each of Bolsen and Corbacho*, with separate tests for whether new categories overlap existing general fields. Specify coder sample and agreement.
3. Results > Associations between reasoning annotations and answers: add model-wise, treatment-adjusted correlations or predictive associations only if supported by complete data. For Corbacho, preserve the three nominal response outcomes. Do not infer causality or mediation from co-generated reasoning.
4. Discussion: distinguish model-conditional behavioral findings from trace-derived correlations and revise the four-case synthesis once estimates are established.
5. Abstract and Conclusion: update only if new reasoning evidence materially changes the top-level message, and retain the boundary between the behavioral four-case corpus and the separate local hidden-state analysis.

## Proposed hypotheses to test, not findings

- Does responsibility attribution, action feasibility, or economic payback co-vary with Bolsen ratings and prompt treatments?
- Does corruption normalization or institutional deterrence correlate with True and refusal outcomes in Corbacho?
- Does self-disclosure management relate to refusal or suppression of affirmative answers, recognizing that its shared direction scale is not meaningful?
- Are apparent realism-prompt effects mediated only *descriptively* by coded statements about instructions or self-presentation, rather than showing an independent process?

## Validity requirements

Keep 0/1/2 causality coding distinct from support-action direction. No direction for Corbacho self-disclosure management pending separate operational justification. Report human coding agreement and category prevalence, specify the exact final executable codebook version, and separate developed examples from held-out validation. Comparisons with the historical annotation set must account for changes in annotator and schema, and cannot assume measurement invariance.

## Figure and table strategy

Reserve capacity for one compact category-by-prompt plot per new case, one category-reliability table (or supplement), and optionally one adjusted-association summary. These are not yet allocated fixed numbering. Behavioral figures remain separate; do not overwrite or renumber existing figures just to make room.
